Our Story

Founded in 2020, Green Nursery started as a passionate local nursery dedicated to cultivating life. Over the years, we have built a loyal community rooted in exceptional plant quality and high-touch customer support. Now we are taking our plant sanctuary online to spread the joy of gardening further.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide sustainably grown, budget-friendly plants alongside high-quality accessories while championing environmentally responsible practices.

Vision Statement

To evolve into a reliable, recognised online plant storefront, making thriving green spaces accessible to plant lovers everywhere.
