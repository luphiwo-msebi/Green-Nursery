Our Story

Founded in 2020, Green Earth Nursery started as a passionate local nursery dedicated to cultivating life. Over the years, we have built a beautiful, loyal community rooted in exceptional plant quality and high-touch customer support. Now, we are taking our plant sanctuary online to spread the joy of gardening even further!

🌱 Indoor Plants 🏡 Garden Plants 🛠️ Gardening Tools 📐 Landscaping Services

Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide exceptional, sustainably grown, and budget-friendly plants alongside high-quality accessories while constantly championing environmentally responsible practices to care for our planet.

Vision Statement

To evolve into a reliable, industry-recognised online plant storefront—demystifying horticulture and making thriving green spaces easily accessible to plant lovers everywhere.
